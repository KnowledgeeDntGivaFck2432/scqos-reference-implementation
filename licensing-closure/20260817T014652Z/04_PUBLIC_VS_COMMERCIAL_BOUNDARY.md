Source commit: `6c156be369b344be99c721c44eeba863eac02414`

## Public layer detected

MIT detected in repository: **True**

The public/open-source layer consists only of code and artifacts already
distributed under their applicable public licenses.

## Paid commercial object

A customer must not be charged merely for permission already granted by MIT.

The commercial value must therefore be explicitly attached to one or more of:

- hosted/managed SCQOS execution
- customer-specific deployment and integration
- private adapters or modules not distributed under MIT
- customer-specific governance policy packs
- signed/frozen governance contracts
- independent verification and evidence production
- production evidence/receipt infrastructure
- support and operational response
- SLA-backed service
- enterprise integration work
- acceptance qualification
- private updates or commercial releases where legally permitted

## HOLD

Machine inspection cannot legally determine which future/private modules are
owned by which party or which commercial services the team elects to sell.

That boundary requires explicit human approval before signature.
